# Prompt Kit — Null Protocol Reconstruction

Use these prompts as a handoff base for Hailuo, Runway, Kling, or Sora.

Global prompt prefix
"CyberChief in a comic-book cyber-defense universe, bold inked outlines, dark cinematic lighting, Batman: The Animated Series meets Tron Legacy meets Mr. Robot, neon cyan and blue accents, mature synthwave atmosphere, heavy shadows, visible face, rectangular glasses, short brown hair with slight recession, blue eyes, salt-and-pepper beard, blue and silver segmented armor, green padlock shield chest emblem, dark blue cape, glowing cyan armor joints"

Scene 1 — Signal in the Dark
Long form:
CyberChief stands on a rain-slick rooftop above a neon network city as a corrupted signal bloom spreads across the skyline, comic-book composition, moody shadows, cyan reflections, cinematic low-angle lens, face fully visible, glasses catching interface light.
Condensed:
CyberChief on neon rooftop, corrupted signal bloom over cyber city, bold comic ink, cyan shadows, face visible, blue/silver armor.

Scene 2 — The Bastion Wakes
Long form:
Inside a dark command center, CyberChief studies cascading alerts across towering displays, cyan holograms reflecting in rectangular glasses, blue/silver segmented armor glowing at the joints, mature synthwave mood, strong silhouette and controlled camera drift.
Condensed:
CyberChief in command center with cyan alert walls, glasses visible, segmented armor glowing, dark cinematic synthwave tone.

Scene 3 — Null Emerges
Long form:
A systems-level corruption tears through the digital environment like living code, with CyberChief bracing against glitching shadow tendrils and fractured light, comic-book shadows, cyan against black, no helmet, no visor, mature high-stakes tone.
Condensed:
Glitch corruption attacks around CyberChief, shadow tendrils, cyan-black contrast, comic-book action, face visible.

Scene 4 — Fortress Protocol
Long form:
CyberChief deploys layered global defenses through sweeping holographic infrastructure maps, low-angle hero framing, blue and silver armor, green chest emblem visible, cyan topology lines spanning Earth, dark cinematic light with crisp comic outlines.
Condensed:
CyberChief activates global defense map, cyan network lines, heroic low angle, armor and green emblem visible.

Scene 5 — Counterstrike
Long form:
CyberChief unleashes a decisive counterstrike through a digital shockwave that rips through enemy command channels, cape trailing behind him, blue/cyan electric burst, bold outlines, dramatic motion, mature cyber-war tone.
Condensed:
CyberChief counterattacks with cyan digital shockwave, cape trailing, bold comic action, enemy systems collapsing.

Scene 6 — The Network Holds
Long form:
At the end of battle, CyberChief stands over a secured widescreen city grid under a synthwave dawn, network lights stable, cape silhouetted, face visible, rectangular glasses, heavy blue/cyan atmosphere, heroic and calm resolution.
Condensed:
CyberChief overlooks secured cyber city at synthwave dawn, face visible, cape silhouette, blue/cyan victory frame.

Platform notes
- Sora: append camera directions like slow push-in, parallax drift, low-angle rise, orbit reveal.
- Runway: keep the condensed prompt under platform limits; pair with image reference.
- Kling: add movement cues like steps forward, turns head, cape trailing, holograms pulsing.
- Hailuo: use CyberChief.png for start/end image reference when supported.
